## Appearance and behavior

The theme follows the device setting until a visitor makes a choice. The choice persists locally across pages and reloads. The theme initialization precedes stylesheet loading to avoid a light-theme flash. With JavaScript disabled, CSS still supports the system's dark preference.

The preloader appears once per tab/session, dismisses after 700 ms, and can be dismissed by a keypress. A separate CSS failsafe hides it after one second. It does not block pointer input, is excluded from the accessibility tree, and is skipped for reduced-motion preferences. Storage failures are caught.
